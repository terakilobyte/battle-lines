Battle Lines — Support

Thanks for playing Battle Lines! Here are quick answers to common parent questions:

How do I restore my Full Adventure purchase on another iPad or iPhone?

Tap Parents in the top-right corner of the Home screen, solve the multiplication problem, and tap Restore Purchases. Family Sharing is supported automatically.

How do I turn on Trace Guide (dotted lines) or mute music?

You can toggle Trace, Background Music, and Sound Effects directly from the top toolbar or inside the Parents Area.
