Battle Lines — Privacy Policy

Zero Data Collected • Zero Ads • Kids Safe

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Battle Lines is a children's drawing, spelling, and math game designed for kids and families. We respect your family's privacy completely.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information. Battle Lines works 100% offline. All drawings, stars, and game settings stay strictly on your device using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults) and are never sent to any server.

2. No Third-Party Ads or Analytics

Battle Lines contains zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero behavioral tracking, and zero social media integrations.

3. In-App Purchases & Parental Gate

Battle Lines offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock additional worlds. All purchase options are protected behind a multiplication Parental Gate and processed solely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Kids Category)

Because we do not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13, Battle Lines complies with COPPA and Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Support Page.
